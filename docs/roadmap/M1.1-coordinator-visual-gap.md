# M1.1 coordinator visual gap after G integration

Evidence: docs/roadmap/M1.1-G-coordinator-artifacts/m11g-fresh-page-reproduced.png, real composited coordinator capture.

Single biggest UI gap: top-right minimap overlaps the open Diagnostics panel. The opaque minimap covers initial diagnostic lines and shares the title/control region. This is a visible layout defect, not a timing or device limitation. Bundle reproduced status and left controls are readable, but the overlapping diagnostics/minimap must not receive final engineering design acceptance.

Bounded design correction for the HUD owner after central integration: keep minimap unobscured and keep all diagnostic controls reachable. Place the diagnostics panel below the minimap or use a deliberate non-overlapping collapsible layout. Cap its height to the viewport above the army rail and make overflow scrollable. Preserve touch input propagation, existing camera commands and performance measurements. Do not hide errors or merely mask the overlap from screenshots. Verify at1280x800 and1194x834 with both panels shown. Keep final screenshot baselines meaningful.
