## Limits and remaining gates

- A person still needs to perform and accept the non-coder walkthrough and judge the editing experience and art. Automated interaction is not human design approval.
- Actual iPad touch, native launch/offline behavior and the device frame-time budget remain unverified. CI compiled the simulator target; it did not prove a physical-device run.
- Q2 ran on Linux Chromium/SwiftShader. Its frame intervals are not GPU timings or iPad measurements. Sampled worker tick p99 was 5.5 ms; composited frame-interval p99 was about 283.3 ms. No device-performance pass is inferred.
- V8 used heap increased by 7,596,168 bytes between the two CDP samples, which include runtime, replay and probe overhead. Stable renderer counts do not prove the absence of every possible heap leak.
- V records bounded missing edge-case coverage and an exceptional injected-callback-throw path. No such throw was observed in the integrated caller. This is a disclosed limitation, not a demonstrated M1.1 blocker.
