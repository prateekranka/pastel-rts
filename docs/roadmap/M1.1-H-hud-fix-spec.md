# M1.1-H Diagnostics HUD layout correction

Status: implementation contract for the final H UI gate.

## Goal

Keep the open Diagnostics panel readable and usable beside the fixed minimap and above the bottom Army Rail at the required 1280x800 and 1194x834 captures.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Diagnostics starts below the 132px minimap with a visible gap. It never occupies the minimap rectangle.
2. Diagnostics ends above the Army Rail with a visible gap. Its body is bounded by the viewport and scrolls when metrics and controls exceed the available height.
3. The header and collapse button remain visible. The button is a practical touch target and exposes `aria-expanded` plus `aria-controls`.
4. Metrics preserve every line, including content errors. Long revision, hash, and asset identifiers wrap at any character and do not widen or clip the panel.
5. The Diagnostics panel consumes pointer input in its own rectangle. Its scroll body permits vertical touch scrolling. No canvas or camera listener is changed.
6. Fresh composited screenshots show both minimap and open Diagnostics at 1280x800 and 1194x834. The evidence does not mask the overlap.

## Non-goals

- Do not change the minimap, Army Rail, camera, simulation, commands, content lifecycle, or performance measurement.
- Do not hide errors, remove metrics, loosen visual thresholds, or edit the root Playwright configuration.

## Implementation boundary

Only `apps/game-web/src/diagnostics/Hud.ts` is changed for the product correction. Existing H runtime tests and evidence remain valid. Any screenshot baseline update must follow actual-versus-expected inspection and keep its existing threshold.
